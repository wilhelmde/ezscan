Help

Print and scan with the printers and scanners on your network - no drivers, no vendor app, no account.

Devices it works with

  • Printers that speak IPP: AirPrint, IPP Everywhere and Mopria printers. Older laser printers on port 9100 (PCL) work too.
  • Scanners that speak eSCL (AirScan) - most current multifunction printers.
  • No scanner at hand? Scan with the camera.

Adding a printer or scanner

  1. Tap Add device on Home. Devices on your Wi-Fi are listed - tap Add.
  2. On another subnet? Enter its IP address or name (192.168.1.40, printer.office.lan, or host:port).
  3. Don't know the address? Search another network with a range such as 10.1.2.0/24 or 10.1.2.10-60 (up to 1,024 addresses).

eZscan Pro asks once for Local Network access. If you said no: Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network › eZscan Pro.

Scanning

Tap Scan on a device. Choose the glass or the feeder (one or both sides), colour, quality and paper size. Reorder or remove pages, then save as PDF or JPEG, share, or print. Pages you haven't saved stay until you save or discard them; if you leave the app first, they go to Photos.

Printing

Tap Print on a device or on Home and choose a document or photo. eZscan Pro sends the format the printer reads best, with copies, colour and two-sided printing, and follows the job until it's printed. The device page shows ink and toner levels.

Your files

Saved scans are in the Documents tab and in the Files app under On My iPhone › eZscan Pro.

Troubleshooting

No devices foundCheck the iPhone is on the same Wi-Fi as the printer and Local Network access is on. Guest networks often hide devices - add the printer by its IP address.
Printer found, scanner notThe device may not offer eSCL (AirScan). Look for an "AirScan" or "eSCL" setting on its web page.
Device on another subnetAdd it by IP address or search the range. Your network must route between the subnets.
"Not answering"The device is off or changed address. Pull down on Home to search again.
